## Manual revision 2 — 15 October 2026

The record above describes the original candidate. This documentation-only revision describes the unchanged released plug-in at `v0.1.0-beta.1`, commit `0efadfbf75a0c76d61e7fcb2a9c509373de6e564`. It does not move that tag or rebuild/replace its binary assets.

The writer personally read the installed `disting-nt-cpp-plugin-writer` skill and its production-workflow guide. The released code's API v14 and pinned SDK govern over the skill's dated v13 baseline. Player instructions were checked against the tag's `src/pop_plugin.cpp`, `src/pop_engine.cpp`, `src/pop_engine.h`, `src/pop_clock_math.h`, `tests/pop_clock_test.cpp` and existing adapter regressions. Installation follows the previously checked official NT manual 1.18, pp69–70. No new minimum-firmware claim is made.

Focused review used two passes: follow the first patch as a reader with basic patching knowledge, then check labels, defaults and behavior against the release. It was a documentation review, not a claim of testing with a teenager or on physical hardware.

| Review finding | Revision |
